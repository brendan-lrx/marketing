For people with elevated blood sugar found at screening

Your screening caught it early — now turn it around with Better Health.

An elevated blood sugar result is an early warning — and the best time to act. LifestyleRx is a virtual, physician-led clinic treating the insulin resistance at the root of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes. Our structured 12-week program brings your blood sugar back into a healthy range through lasting lifestyle change — fully funded by provincial healthcare, at no cost to you.

How the 12-week program works
Physician visits
One-on-one care from a LifestyleRx doctor
Group sessions
Weekly, led by a physician & dietitian
Videos & lessons
An interactive weekly curriculum
Personal reports
Lab insights and progress tracking
~30 hours over 12 immersive weeks, followed by a 12-month maintenance phase for lasting change.
What the program helps you do
✓Lower your HbA1c and stabilize daily blood sugar
✓Achieve sustainable weight loss and protect heart health
✓Stop prediabetes from progressing to type 2 diabetes
✓Build lasting habits in nutrition, activity, sleep and stress
✓Often reverse prediabetes entirely through lifestyle change
